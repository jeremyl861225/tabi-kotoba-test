彈彈坦克 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 3 日

「彈彈坦克」（以下稱「本 App」）是一款單機與連線對戰的玩具坦克遊戲。我們重視你的隱私：本 App 不需要註冊帳號，不蒐集、不儲存、也不販售你的個人資料。

存在你手機裡的資料

遊戲進度、星星、金幣、車庫裡的坦克、設定與你取的名字，只存在你自己的裝置上，不會上傳給我們。刪除 App 就會一併刪除。

連線對戰

使用「連線對戰」時，為了讓你和朋友的裝置互相找到，本 App 會透過 Supabase 的即時通道交換連線所需的資訊（房號、你在遊戲裡取的名字、網路連線資訊），之後雙方裝置直接連線對戰。這些資訊只在連線當下傳遞，我們不會保存。和你同一個房間的玩家會看到你取的名字。

App 內購買

購買金幣由 Apple 的 App Store 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資料。買到的金幣只記在你的裝置上。

不做的事

  • 沒有廣告，也沒有追蹤你的廣告識別碼。
  • 沒有分析工具、不記錄你的使用行為。
  • 不使用你的位置、通訊錄、相機、麥克風或照片。

兒童

本 App 適合全年齡遊玩，且不蒐集任何人的個人資料，包括兒童。購買金幣時，若裝置開啟了「購買前詢問」，需要家長同意。

Bounce Tanks Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Bounce Tanks (“the App”) is a single-player and online toy-tank game. The App does not require an account and does not collect, store, or sell your personal data.

Data on your device

Your progress, stars, coins, garage tanks, settings and the name you choose are stored only on your own device and are never uploaded to us. Deleting the App deletes them.

Online play

When you use online play, the App exchanges the information needed for your devices to find each other (room code, the name you chose in the game, and network connection details) through a Supabase real-time channel; the devices then connect to each other directly. This information is passed along only while connecting and is not kept by us. Players in the same room can see the name you chose.

In-app purchases

Coin purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store. We never receive your payment details. Purchased coins are recorded only on your device.

What we don’t do

  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics and no tracking of how you use the App.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children. If “Ask to Buy” is turned on, coin purchases need a parent’s approval.
