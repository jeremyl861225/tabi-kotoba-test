Typeday Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Typeday doesn’t collect any of your data. What you enter stays on your iPhone and in the backup location you choose yourself. We don’t run a server and can’t see your data.

Data We Collect

None. Typeday needs no account, has no ads, and uses no analytics, tracking tools or third-party SDKs. The app never sends what you enter over the internet, and it doesn’t ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library. The only exception is App Lock: when you turn it on, iPhone asks once whether Typeday may use Face ID.

Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Data in the app: your routines, events, tasks, categories, check-offs and settings are stored in Typeday’s own folder on this iPhone (the widgets read from there too). Daily copies from the last 7 days are kept automatically in case a file gets damaged.
  • Widgets and the Lock Screen: widgets read data directly from this iPhone, never over the internet. Widgets or the weekly wallpaper on your Lock Screen show your items even while iPhone is locked, so decide for yourself whether to use them.
  • Auto Backup: once you turn it on, the app saves a backup file every day to the folder you pick in Files and keeps the last 30. If you choose iCloud Drive, the backups are stored in your own iCloud account and kept by Apple; we can’t access them.
  • Export and import: files are written or read only when you tap Export or Import. Where a file goes and who you share it with is up to you.
  • Backups and exports aren’t encrypted: they are plain text files (JSON), so anyone who gets the file can read it. Keep them safe.
  • Wallpaper background: if you pick a photo for the Lock Screen weekly wallpaper, the app receives only that one photo and stores a smaller copy in the app. It can’t see the other photos in your library.
  • iPhone backups: your iPhone’s iCloud or computer backups may include this app’s data, handled according to your iPhone settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

Face ID / Touch ID Lock

With App Lock on, unlocking is verified by iOS. The app only learns whether it succeeded and never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode. App Lock protects only the app itself; widgets and the Lock Screen weekly wallpaper are drawn by iPhone and still show your items.

In-App Purchases

The Full Version is paid for through Apple’s In-App Purchase and processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details, Apple Account or name. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple Account has bought the Full Version, to decide which features to turn on. Your download and purchase history is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Crash Reports

If you turn on “Share With App Developers” in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share crash and performance reports with us that don’t identify you. We use them only to fix bugs. Sharing is your choice in iPhone Settings, and you can turn it off at any time.

External Links

Links you add to items open in Safari or the matching app. Once opened, those websites or apps are covered by their own privacy policies.

Deleting Your Data

  • Items you delete in the app are removed from your iPhone.
  • Deleting Typeday deletes all of its data, including the data used by the widgets and the daily copies.
  • Backup files in the folder you chose aren’t removed when you delete the app. If you don’t need them, delete them yourself in the Files app.
  • We have never received your data, so there is nothing for us to provide, change or delete. Your data is entirely under your control.

Children

Typeday doesn’t collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its “Last updated” date. If the app adds a feature that changes how data is handled, we’ll update this policy before it ships and mention it in the app’s release notes.
