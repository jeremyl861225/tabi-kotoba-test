描述（上限 4000 字）

Typeday 是一本打字機風格的手帳：每天、每週要做的事，一次性的行程，還有要花好幾天完成的任務，都整齊排在同一張紙上。打勾的時候，就像在打字機上敲下一個 [x]。

■ 四個頁面
・重複排程：每日、每週、每月（例如每月 15 號、每月第二個星期三），或自訂每幾天、每幾週、每幾個月。下一次是哪天自動算好，點狀節奏條一眼看出接下來四週哪幾天要做。
・月曆：每天的小記號告訴你那天有什麼；點一天，當天要做的事全部列出來，直接打勾。
・近期任務：有截止日的大任務拆成子任務，做完一步勾一步，完成度一看就知道。
・未完成：過去沒勾到的事自動集中到這裡，可以補勾，或把這一次挪到別天。

■ 小地方
・重要的事標成「重大事件」，在清單裡特別顯眼。
・類別用顏色區分；每件事都能寫敘述、放相關連結、加子事項。
・兩種外觀：打字機（等寬字、格線紙、中括號勾選框）與糖果紙（圓角卡片、手繪螢光筆、會彈跳的勾選框）；淺色、深色或跟著系統。
・第一次打開有互動教學，跟著點一遍就會用。

■ 資料只在你這邊
・不用註冊帳號，沒有廣告，不追蹤；我們沒有伺服器，看不到你的資料。
・自動備份：每天自動存一份到你選的資料夾（建議選 iCloud 雲碟），保留最近 30 份。刪掉 App、換手機，資料都還在。
・隨時可以匯出成 JSON 檔，也能從備份檔匯入還原。
・可以用 Face ID／Touch ID 鎖住 App，別人拿到你的手機也打不開。

■ 完整版（App 內購買，一次買斷，沒有訂閱）
・桌面小工具：大、中、小三種，以及鎖定畫面三種；點某一天直接打開那天。
・鎖定畫面週曆桌布：搭配「捷徑」每天自動換上本週週曆，也可以用自己的照片當底。
・糖果紙外觀。
除了上面三項，其他功能都免費，事項數量也沒有限制。

Description（上限 4000 字）

Typeday is a typewriter-style planner. Things you do every day or every week, one-off events, and tasks that take a few days all line up neatly on one page. Checking something off feels like typing an [x].

■ FOUR PAGES
• Recurring: daily, weekly, monthly (like the 15th, or the 2nd Wednesday), or every few days, weeks or months. Each routine knows when it’s due next, and a dotted strip shows the days it falls on over the next four weeks.
• Calendar: small marks show what’s on each day. Tap a day to see everything due and check it off right there.
• Upcoming: break big tasks with due dates into subtasks and check them off one step at a time.
• Pending: anything you missed collects here. Check it off late, or move just that one time to another day.

■ THE DETAILS
• Mark important items so they stand out in your lists.
• Color-coded categories. Add notes, links and checklists to anything.
• Two looks: Typewriter (monospaced type, ruled paper, bracket checkboxes) and Candy (rounded cards, hand-drawn highlighter, bouncy checkboxes). Light, dark, or match your iPhone.
• Optional Face ID or Touch ID lock.
• A one-minute interactive tutorial the first time you open it.

■ YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• No account, no ads, no tracking. We don’t run a server and can’t see your data.
• Auto Backup saves a copy every day to a folder you choose (we recommend iCloud Drive) and keeps the last 30. Delete the app or switch phones, and your data is still there.
• Export to JSON anytime, and restore from any backup file.

■ FULL VERSION (in-app purchase, one-time, no subscription)
• Home Screen widgets in large, medium and small sizes, plus three for the Lock Screen. Tap a day to open it.
• Lock Screen weekly wallpaper: with Shortcuts, this week’s calendar is swapped in every day, on top of your own photo if you like.
• The Candy look.
Everything else is free, with no limit on how many items you add.

Typeday is available in English and Traditional Chinese.
